Born from the market, finished at the table

Savanna Table began as a family idea: cook the dishes of West Africa the way they are made at home, with the care of a fine dining kitchen. Our chefs grind their own pepper blends, smoke their own fish, and buy greens, tomatoes and peppers from growers they know by name.

Every meal is built on slow-cooked stews, open-flame grilling and spices that carry a story. Our hosts treat each guest like family, so a table here is never rushed.

Whether it is a quiet dinner, a birthday, or an evening in the lounge, we aim to make it one you remember.
